Yes Boss privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version

Yes Boss: Kebab Shop Tycoon does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved game, settings (volume, music choice, language) and legacy stars are kept only on your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for any permissions beyond what Android needs to run the game.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't sell or share data, because we don't have any.

Google Play

Google Play handles purchases and downloads under Google's own privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details.

Children

Yes Boss is rated 12+ because of night-life themes. It doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if we add cloud saves, we'll update this page and the date above before the update goes out.
