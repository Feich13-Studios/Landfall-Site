What you'll be doing

Plan every shift

Choose the spit size, prices, meal deals, stock and the rota. The forecast tells you about football nights, gigs and payday Fridays.

Run the night live

Customers order at the counter, wait and collect at the pass. Jump on the till, break up fights, call taxis and send for a cash and carry run.

Staff with personality

Grill masters, night owls, hotheads and chatterboxes. Some are mates and some clash. Keep them happy or they'll hand in their notice.

Rivals that fight back

Price wars, poached staff, flyers on your street and fake reviews. See one off and a tougher challenger turns up.

Build an empire

Nine UK cities, a festival pitch, a stadium van, a seaside kiosk and an airport unit. Then franchise across the country.

Sell up, go again

Cash in your empire for legacy stars and start a new one in a different city, with bigger perks each time.
