From the van to the chain

Three shops, twelve fit-outs, five cities and one very long night.

Gas Grill → Proper Grill → Rotisserie → Fitted-out Van

The Backstreet

How you'll play

Plan each morning, trade through the night, learn from the books.

Size the spit

The doner cooks all day and whatever's left gets binned. Read the forecast, the weather and the day of the week, and get it right.

More than kebabs

Wraps, burgers, salads, munchy boxes, sides and cans. One customer buys a can, the next buys eight kebabs for their mates.

Staff with personalities

Fast Hands, Chatty, Spotless, Hothead, Sticky Fingers. Keep morale up and they'll shout "Yes boss!" Let it slip and they ring in sick.

Night life

Chucking-out time, hen dos, drunk singers, the odd scrap in the queue, blue lights, taxis at the rank and chips on the pavement.

Run a real business

Prices, stock, suppliers, rotas, hygiene inspections, late licences, delivery apps and reputation. Three days in the red and you're bust.

Made to come back to

A challenge every shift, 24 achievements, lifetime stats, a full UK year of events and a hardcore mode that wipes the save.

Five cities, five rivals

Every city has its own rhythm, and a rival who wants your customers.

Stoke

Grill Sergeant

Where it starts. Steady all year, lively on football nights.

Leeds

Freshly Shaved

Students: huge in term, dead in summer. Freshers' week is gold.

Blackpool

Golden Spit

Summer and the illuminations boom. Winter is bleak. Hen dos galore.

Newcastle

Dirty Dave's

The biggest late rush in the game. And the most trouble.

London

Spitfire

Huge footfall and big spends, with eye-watering rent.
