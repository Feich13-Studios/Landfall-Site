Privacy Policy — Main Squeeze

Effective date: 09/11/2026

Feich13 Studios ("we", "us") makes Main Squeeze (the "App"), a single-player lemonade stand tycoon game for Android. This policy explains, in plain terms, what information the App uses and what it doesn't.

The short version

Main Squeeze doesn't collect personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your progress is saved on your own device. If your version of the App offers Google Play Games and you choose to use it, Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

To save your progress, the App stores the following on your device:

  • Career and challenge saves: day, cash, stands, upgrades, stock, recipe, regulars and your career statistics.
  • Your character: the name and stand name you type in, and how your character looks. These can be anything you like, and don't need to be your real name.
  • Progress: achievements, challenge stars and your daily streak.
  • Settings: sound, language, text size, colour-blind colours, left-handed layout, customer chatter and tutorial tips.

None of this is sent to us. We don't run any servers, and the App doesn't send it to any third party.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your real name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, advertising ID, device identifiers or any other personal information. The App has no account system.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The App contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDKs. Apart from optional Google Play Games (below), it makes no network requests and works fully offline. If this ever changes, we'll update this policy before the change is released.

Google Play Games (optional)

Some versions of the App let you sign in to Google Play Games to sync achievements and back up your career save. This is optional, and the App works the same without it. If you use it:

  • Google receives your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), the achievements you unlock and, if you use cloud save, your career save file.
  • This data is stored and processed by Google, not by us, under the Google Privacy Policy. It's encrypted in transit.
  • We can't see your Google account details.
  • You can delete this data at any time from your Play Games profile or your Google account.

Purchases

Main Squeeze is a paid app with no in-app purchases. Google Play processes your payment, and we never see your payment details.

Permissions

The App doesn't ask for any special permissions. It doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Children's privacy

The App doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Sharing and sale of data

We don't sell, rent or share personal data. The App doesn't collect any to share.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your saves stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete your career from Settings → Delete save. Uninstalling the App removes everything stored on the device. Data held by Google Play Games can be deleted as described above.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data about you, there's normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or concern, contact us and we'll help. If you're in the UK, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). In the EU or EEA, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics and Mailchimp tracking, but only if you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you join our mailing list, Mailchimp stores your email address so we can send studio updates, and you can unsubscribe from any email. The website is separate from the App.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes, we'll update this page and the effective date before the change reaches the App.
