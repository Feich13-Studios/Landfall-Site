How you'll play

Plan each morning, trade through the day, learn from the results.

Read the weather

Four seasons and a daily forecast that’s usually right. Heatwaves bring queues, rain empties the street, and cold days call for hot lemon & honey.

Perfect your recipe

Lemons for tang, sugar for sweet, ice for cold. Nine kinds of customer, from kids to joggers to executives, all want something different.

Price it right

A live price guide shows who’ll pay what. Change the price mid-day as the crowd builds. Too high and they walk, too low and you’re giving it away.

Regulars, rivals and critics

Win regulars who come back daily and ask for their favourite. Word of mouth spreads, rival stands move in, and a secret food critic might be in the queue.

Run a real business

Staff, supplier deals, bulk orders, weather insurance, lemon price contracts and bank loans. Keep your kit serviced or it breaks down mid-rush.

Manage your stock

Ice melts and lemons spoil. Grow from the corner shop to the wholesaler to your own lemon grove, or hire a stock manager to do it for you.

Make it yours

Create your character, name your stand, decorate with bunting, lights and parasols, and adopt a stand dog or cat.

Challenges and sandbox

Six challenge scenarios rated in stars, plus a sandbox with unlimited cash to build your dream stand.

Play your way

Relaxed, Normal or Hardcore careers, 28 achievements, a daily streak bonus and a full career stats screen.
