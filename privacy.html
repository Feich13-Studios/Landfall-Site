This policy explains what information the App does — and does not — collect, in plain terms. ## What we collect LANDFALL runs primarily on your device, and you do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play the game. The App stores your gameplay data locally on your device only, including: - Season and storm progress - Achievement unlocks - Settings preferences (music, sound effects, haptics) This local data stays on your device. It is never transmitted to us, to Feich13 Studios' servers (we don't operate any), or to any third party. **Leaderboards (optional).** LANDFALL includes an optional leaderboard feature powered by Google Play Games Services. If you choose to sign in and submit a score, the following is sent to Google, not to us: - Your Google Play Games identity (Gamertag and avatar), linked to your existing Google Account - The score you're submitting This only happens if you actively choose to sign in and use the leaderboard — nothing is sent anywhere if you don't. We never see or receive this data ourselves; it's handled entirely between your device and Google's own servers. See "Leaderboard data" below for details on how this is handled and how to delete it. ## What we don't collect We do not collect your name, email address, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or any other personal information. LANDFALL has no account system of its own — the only sign-in available is the optional Google Play Games Services leaderboard described above, which uses your existing Google Account rather than creating any new account with us. ## Leaderboard data If you sign in to the optional leaderboard: - **What's collected:** your Google Play Games Gamertag/User ID and the scores you submit. - **Who processes it:** Google, as part of Google Play Games Services — not Feich13 Studios. We don't operate any servers of our own, and this data never passes through or is stored by us. - **Why:** solely to provide the leaderboard feature — showing your rank against other players. - **Deleting this data:** you can delete your Play Games data at any time through your [Google Play Games profile](https://play.google.com/games/profile) or your Google Account settings. Since we never hold this data ourselves, there's nothing on our end to separately delete. - **Data in transit:** Google Play Games Services encrypts this data in transit. ## Analytics, advertising, and third-party services LANDFALL does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs). No data is shared with advertising networks because the App does not display ads. The one exception is the optional Google Play Games Services leaderboard described above — this is a core platform feature for the leaderboard, not analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it only activates if you choose to sign in. **If our data practices change further** — for example, if a future update adds crash reporting, analytics, or ads — this policy will be updated before that update is released, with the change noted below and reflected in the app's Data Safety declaration. ## This website's own analytics This section is separate from everything above, which describes the LANDFALL **app**. This landfallgame.com **website** (the page you're reading right now) uses two third-party tracking scripts, both gated behind the cookie consent banner below — neither loads unless you click Accept: - **Google Analytics** — understands visitor traffic: how many people visit, which pages they view, and roughly where visitors are located. - **Mailchimp's Connected Site tracking** — tracks visitor behavior on this site to support Mailchimp's own email automation features, since this site's newsletter signup is powered by Mailchimp. Both use cookies and similar technologies to do this. This is separate from the App itself, which — as described above — collects no analytics of its own, beyond the optional leaderboard. This site shows a cookie consent banner on first visit, and Google Analytics does not load at all unless you click Accept — declining (or simply not choosing) means no analytics script runs. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, which will show the banner again on your next visit. You can also opt out of Google Analytics specifically via [Google's opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). ## Permissions The App may request device permissions such as vibration/haptic feedback, used solely to provide in-game feedback (for example, when collecting an energy pickup). These permissions do not grant access to personal data. ## Children's privacy LANDFALL is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The App collects no personal data from any user unless they actively choose to sign in to the optional leaderboard — this applies equally regardless of age. ## Data sharing and sale We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties ourselves, because we do not operate any servers or hold any personal data to share. The one exception is the optional leaderboard, where data is sent directly from your device to Google, not to us, and solely to provide that feature — see "Leaderboard data" above. ## Data retention and deletion Local gameplay data (season progress, achievements, settings) is stored only on your device, and you control it directly — uninstalling the App removes it. There is no server-side account or data for us to delete on your behalf, because we don't operate any servers. If you've used the optional leaderboard, your Gamertag and scores are held by Google, not by us — see "Leaderboard data" above for how to delete that data directly through Google. ## Changes to this policy If our data practices change, we'll update this page and revise the effective date above.
