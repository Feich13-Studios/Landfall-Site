Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29th July 2026

Feich13 Studios ("we," "us") develops LANDFALL (the "App"), a single-player hurricane simulation game for Android. This policy explains what information the App does — and does not — collect, in plain terms.

What we collect

LANDFALL runs entirely on your device. You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play.

The App stores your gameplay data locally on your device only, including:

  • Season and storm progress
  • Achievement unlocks
  • Settings preferences (music, sound effects, haptics)

This data stays on your device. It is never transmitted to us, to Feich13 Studios' servers (we don't operate any), or to any third party.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, email address, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or any other personal information. The App does not have a login system, so there is no account data of any kind to collect.

Analytics, advertising, and third-party services

LANDFALL does not currently integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs). No data is shared with advertising networks because the App does not display ads.

If this changes — for example, if a future update adds crash reporting, analytics, or ads — this policy will be updated before that update is released, with the change noted below and reflected in the app's Data Safety declaration.

Permissions

The App may request device permissions such as vibration/haptic feedback, used solely to provide in-game feedback (for example, when collecting an energy pickup). These permissions do not grant access to personal data.

Children's privacy

LANDFALL is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects no personal data from any user, this applies equally regardless of age.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties, because we do not collect any personal data to share.

Data retention and deletion

Since all gameplay data is stored locally on your device, you control it directly. Uninstalling the App removes this data. There is no server-side account or data for us to delete on your behalf, because none exists.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we'll update this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: feich13studios@gmail.com

LANDFALL is developed by Feich13 Studios. This policy applies to the LANDFALL Android application only.
