About the Studio

Small team, big simulations

Feich13 Studios is a one-person studio based in the UK, building games in Unity in the evenings and at weekends.

Every game starts with a real system that's fun to push around, whether that's sea surface temperatures and wind shear or the economics of a lemonade stand on a rainy Tuesday, and turns it into something you can learn, master and share.

You buy the game once. There are no ads, no energy timers and no shops inside the game. That's the promise for everything we make.
